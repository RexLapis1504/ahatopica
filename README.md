# AHATOPICA · LAUNCH ROOM — Boardroom Wars: The EV Race

A boardroom game for an MBA *Management of Technology & Innovation* class (Session 6:
stages of technology development; factors affecting technology exploitation decisions).

Live: **https://ahatopica.pages.dev**

## What is here
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `data.js`, `game.js`: the **sample play**, a solo, browser-only build.
  You sit on one board; bots play every other seat and company.
- `audio/`: event sound cues cut from the team's own track *Ex Nihilo*.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server.
- `?auto=1`: watch mode (bots play every seat)
- `?speed=10`: faster timers for testing

## Notes
- This is a classroom simulation. The company cases are simplified from public reports, and deals between
  boards are in-game what-ifs. No company is affiliated with it, and no logos are used.
- The boss, "The Closed Plug", is fictional. The personas are original.
- No student data is stored in this repository.
